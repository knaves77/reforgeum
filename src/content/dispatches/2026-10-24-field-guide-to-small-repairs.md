You can't fix the world before breakfast, but you can sew a button back on.

1. Pick one thing that's broken and small enough to hold.
2. Fix it badly. Badly is still fixed.
3. Notice how your hands feel afterwards.
4. Repeat tomorrow.

> Repair is a quiet kind of protest.
