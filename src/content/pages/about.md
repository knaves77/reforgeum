title: About this board
dek: Who pins these up, and why.
---

This board is for people who are patching themselves up emotionally and heading back out into the world to earn their bread and butter.

Every day a new notice goes up. It's one person's view of the life and times of this pre-apocalypse era: what's happening, what it feels like, and how we keep going anyway.

Two kinds of notices get pinned here. **Dispatches** are the long ones: essays and ramblings. **Scraps** are the short ones: notes scribbled down and pinned up, read right on the board.

Take what you need. Leave what you can.
